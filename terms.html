1. The service

VeriShot lets you capture photos, videos and live meetings and seal them with a device signature and an RFC 3161 timestamp. A sealed credential can later be verified by anyone holding the file and the credential.

2. What a seal proves, and what it does not

A seal proves that a file with a given hash existed at the time recorded by the timestamp authority, and that the credential was signed by the key on your device. It does not prove that the scene depicted is true, nor that the device was not tampered with before capture. We provide cryptographic evidence, not a legal conclusion.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Keep your device and its signing key safe. If the key is lost, previously issued credentials remain verifiable but no new ones can be signed with it.
  • Only capture what you are entitled to capture. Recording laws differ by jurisdiction; you are responsible for complying with them.
  • Do not use the app to fabricate, alter or misrepresent evidence.
